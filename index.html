How to play

  1. Read the visitor request shown in the request panel.
  2. Select the correct action: Borrow, Return, Renew, or Fine.
  3. Each correct answer adds points and increases your combo.
  4. Each wrong answer reduces 4 seconds from your timer.
  5. Finish the 60-second session with the highest score possible.
